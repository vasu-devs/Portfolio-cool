JustHireMe / Open-source desktop

A job search with
the person in control.

Job search shouldn’t mean handing your career to a black box. I built a local-first workbench that brings discovery, explainable matching, and application drafts into one place. The aim is simple: help people understand a recommendation before they act on it.

Built with Tauri, React, and Python. Supported by graph and vector retrieval. 2,200+ GitHub stars.

Svara / On-device voice

The best interface
can be your voice.

Dictation gets interesting when it leaves the demo window. Svara puts speech at the cursor, inside the tools you already use. I worked on the unglamorous details that make that feel natural: the first word, interrupted recordings, cleanup, and text that lands where it should.

Local speech recognition, application-aware insertion, and recovery for unfinished dictation. Built for Windows.

Dreamer / Personal research system

Give a rough idea
somewhere to go.

A promising thought deserves more than a forgotten note. Dreamer turns it into a workspace where agents research, plan, and challenge assumptions. Their edits arrive as proposals, so a person can inspect the evidence and decide what belongs in the document.

The deeper work is in the execution: durable jobs, recovery, usage accounting, and a clear boundary between a convincing story and a supported claim.
