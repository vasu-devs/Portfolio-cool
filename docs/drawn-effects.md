# Drawn companion effects

The rejected scaleX SVG extensions are replaced by thirteen RGBA four-frame sheets. Each sequence keeps its square aspect ratio. Local impacts stay near the actor; barriers stay upright; target effects form at the click. Projectiles have bounded travel, with no stretching. These are stylized adaptations, not traced manga panels or exact anime frames.
