# Bleach title-card typography study

## Current direction
The prior official-website serif interpretation and subsequent handwritten interpretation were rejected by the user. Neither is the active direction.

Inspected actual reference frames:
- Official episode 1 eyecatch: https://bleach-anime.com/assets/img/keyword/01.jpg
- Official Gotei 13 eyecatch: https://bleach-anime.com/assets/img/keyword/12.jpg
- The Fire title-card capture: https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bleach/images/1/18/Ep372TitleCard.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/1200?cb=20221114191606&path-prefix=en

Observed: heavy tightly condensed English display text; title-card lettering in sentence case as well as all caps; dramatic size contrast; small numbered labels; hard-edged division; black/white with saturated accents. These differ materially from the softer Barlow and handwritten experiments. Exact production font identity is not verified.

Adaptation: self-hosted Anton is a visual substitute for heavy title lettering, not a claim of the exact production face. IBM Plex Sans provides readable supporting text. Section headings are 36–48 px, project names 30–34 px, modal titles 30–36 px. Small numbered chapter markers and a limited magenta accent recall eyecatches while keeping the approved realm backgrounds. Preserve the existing Bleach wordmark. Remove handwritten greeting, doodle, underline and all serif prose. Keep sentence case, respecting earlier user feedback against indiscriminate all caps.

Validation: rendered desktop and mobile in both themes; page and modal horizontal overflow checks passed at 390 and 1280 px; modal-layout and film-modal regression checks passed. Fonts and license files sourced from google/fonts (SIL OFL).

Status: local preview, not published.
